How the Geometry & Packing Math Works

Pillowed Ziploc Bag Volume: A flat rectangular pouch (width W, usable height H below the zipper track) does not inflate into a box. Using Mylar-balloon / biconvex lens inflation geometry, maximum enclosed volume approaches V ≈ 0.21 × W × H × min(W, H), derated to 85% so the zipper seal holds without bursting.
Packing Fractions (η): Standard 1×1 stud pitch is 8.0 mm × 8.0 mm × 9.6 mm (0.614 cm³). Loose random pouring achieves η ≈ 0.44–0.58 depending on aspect ratio (long plates tangle more than round tiles), whereas disciplined stacking achieves η ≈ 0.76–0.86 inside rigid boxes and η ≈ 0.68–0.78 inside curved bags.
